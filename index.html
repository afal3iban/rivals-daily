How to play

Guess the hero. Every guess shows how close each attribute is.

  • Green — exact match.
  • Orange — partly right. Some teams (or roles) overlap, but not all of them.
  • Red — no match.
  • ↑ / ↓ on the year — the answer came out later / earlier.

One hero per day, the same one for everyone. Hit Share when you finish and paste the grid into Discord.
