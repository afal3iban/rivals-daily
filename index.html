How to play

A random hero is picked. Guess who it is — every guess shows how close each attribute is.

  • Green — exact match.
  • Orange — partly right. Some teams (or roles) overlap, but not all of them.
  • Red — no match.
  • ↑ / ↓ on the year — the answer came out later / earlier.

Type a name or click a hero in the list. Unlimited guesses, unlimited rounds — hit New hero any time.
